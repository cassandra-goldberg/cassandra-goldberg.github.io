Hello! My name is Cassandra (Casey) Goldberg. I am a third-year computer science PhD student at the University of Pennsylvania. I study how AI systems represent and reason with information, developing theory that makes their internal computations more interpretable and reliable. In collaboration with surgeons, I bring these ideas to clinically grounded AI systems whose evidence and decisions can be audited. I am fortunate to be advised by [Eric Wong](https://riceric22.github.io/) and to work as part of his [Brachio Lab](https://brachiolab.github.io/).

I previously earned my BA in computer science and physics at Bowdoin College. There, I researched statistically-sound deep learning techniques for synthetic aperture radar (SAR) image segmentation under the mentorship of [Jeová Farias](https://jeovafarias.github.io/).
